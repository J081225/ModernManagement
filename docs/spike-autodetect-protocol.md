# Language Test Line — Caller Sheet

**Call: +1 (313) 631-8389** · Takes about 3 minutes · Your voice is
transcribed for quality testing only. Thank you!

**How it works:** A test system answers in English and says
"Recorded. Please read your next line." after each thing you say.
That's normal — read your lines one at a time, pausing after each.
It will NOT understand you or book anything — it only listens.

## Your six lines

Read the lines for YOUR language. Speak naturally — the way you'd
really talk on the phone, not slowly or extra-clearly.

**If you speak Arabic** (please tell us your dialect: Levantine /
Yemeni / Iraqi / other):
1. مرحبا، كيفكم؟ هاي أول مرة بتصل فيكم.
2. بدي احجز موعد قصة شعر يوم السبت الصبح إذا في مجال.
3. قديش سعر صبغة الشعر عندكم؟
4. *(code-switching — say it exactly like this)*: هاي، بدي أعمل
   appointment لبكرا، بس same time متل المرة الماضية إذا ممكن.
5. *(turn on a TV or stand in the kitchen, then)*: مرحبا، بدي إسأل
   إذا عندكم مواعيد فاضية نهاية الأسبوع.
6. *(fast, like you're in a hurry)*: يعطيكم العافية بس سؤال سريع —
   لوين بتوصل عندكم مواقف السيارات؟

**If you speak Spanish:**
1. Hola, ¿qué tal? Es la primera vez que llamo.
2. Quisiera agendar una cita para un corte de pelo el sábado por la mañana.
3. ¿Cuánto cuesta el tinte de cabello con ustedes?
4. *(code-switching)*: Hola, necesito un appointment para mañana,
   same time que la última vez, si se puede.
5. *(with background noise)*: Hola, quería preguntar si tienen citas
   disponibles el fin de semana.
6. *(fast)*: Una pregunta rápida — ¿dónde puedo estacionar cuando llegue?

**If you speak English (control group):**
1. Hi there, how's it going? First time calling.
2. I'd like to book a haircut for Saturday morning if you have anything.
3. How much is hair coloring with you guys?
4. Hey, can I get an appointment tomorrow — same time as last visit?
5. *(with background noise)*: Hi, just wondering if you have any
   weekend openings.
6. *(fast)*: Quick question — where do I park when I get there?
