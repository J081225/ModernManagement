SMS Opt-In Proof

The consent surfaces used by businesses on the Modern Management platform (R2 LABS LLC). This page and both images are publicly viewable — no login required.

1. The end-user opt-in form (live)

Publicly reachable at modernmanagementapp.com/sms-opt-in. The consent checkbox is unchecked by default and the submit button stays disabled until it is checked. Each opt-in is recorded with the exact wording, date, and phone number. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

2. The in-app owner intake gate

When a business adds a customer's phone number inside the platform, saving is blocked until the business confirms — via the same unchecked-by-default checkbox wording — that the customer agreed to receive these messages.
