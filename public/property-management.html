What it does

Built for landlords who manage their own portfolio.

Property records.

Properties, units, leases, contacts, vendors. AI organizes everything in one place — and answers any question about it in plain English.

Tenant communication.

Inbound SMS, email, and voicemail handled by AI. Routine messages handled automatically. Emergencies surfaced and escalated within seconds.

Rent and finances.

Track payments, late status, and invoices. AI drafts late notices, processes inbound payment confirmations from email, and reconciles automatically.

Maintenance.

Tickets come in by SMS, email, or voicemail. AI categorizes urgency, suggests vendors, schedules visits, and follows up. You only see what needs you.

Reports and insights.

Ask for a quarterly report and get it. Vacancy, collections, maintenance costs, occupancy trends. Natural language in, accountant-ready out.

Daily briefing.

What changed since yesterday. What needs your attention. What you can ignore. Surfaced every morning.

The honest answer

You're worried AI will mess something up.

So are we. That's why every action that touches a tenant — a late notice, a broadcast, a maintenance reply, a refund — waits for your approval. The AI proposes; you say yes. You're the boss; it's the assistant.

Beyond approvals, you teach the AI everything it needs to know about your business in plain English. Hours. Policies. Vendor preferences. The way you talk to tenants. The AI reads everything you wrote before it responds to anything. The more you teach it, the better it gets.
