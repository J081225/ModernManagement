How it works

Three steps to get started.

1

You sign up.

Pick your business type. We provision your own phone number for customer texts. Five minutes.

2

You teach the AI.

Write your hours, services, policies, and how you want things done — in plain English. The AI reads it before responding to anything.

3

The AI handles the rest.

Inbound texts. Bookings. Reports. Reminders. Inventory. Vendor messages. Daily summaries of what matters. The busy work runs itself; you decide what to do next.

Why we built this

This isn't a scheduling tool with AI bolted on. It's an AI brain that runs your business.

Most software adds AI as a feature. We built ours the other way: AI is the engine, and the features — scheduling, records, reports, inventory, communication — are what it does. Every customer text, every booking, every report, every reorder message goes through an AI that knows your business because you taught it. The result is software that doesn't just record what happened. It reads, decides, and acts.
