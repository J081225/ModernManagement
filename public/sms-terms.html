SMS Program Terms & Privacy

Last updated: Aug 16, 2026

Modern Management's SMS program sends transactional and service messages only — appointment reminders and confirmations, customer-service replies, one-time verification codes, and payment links and receipts. Messages are sent by each business that uses the Modern Management platform to that business's own customers, with whom the business has an existing relationship; Modern Management operates the platform on the business's behalf. We do not send marketing or promotional messages through this program. We do not share or sell mobile phone numbers, mobile information, or SMS opt-in and consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out (you will receive one confirmation and no further messages) and HELP for help. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

What these messages are

The messages sent through this program are strictly transactional and service-related, tied to an appointment, an account, or a payment. They are business-initiated in response to a customer's inquiry or booking. No advertising, promotional, or marketing content is sent on this program.

Consent

Consumers provide their mobile number to a business when they contact it (by call, text, or in person) or when booking an appointment, and in doing so consent to receive service messages about their appointment and account. A consent disclosure is shown where the number is collected. Messages are sent only to customers who have an existing relationship with the business.

Opt-out and help

You can stop messages at any time by replying STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT. You will receive a single confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply; message frequency varies.

Privacy — mobile information is never shared for marketing

Mobile information — including mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in and consent data — is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, and is never sold or rented. Text-message opt-in and consent data is not shared with any third parties. Numbers are used only to deliver the transactional and service messages described above. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Supported carriers

SMS messages are supported on major U.S. carriers, including AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile, and most regional carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
