Grow your business
Set up the tools that help you attract and keep more customers. Add your links below, and learn the safe, effective ways to grow.

Why reviews matter: When someone searches for a business like yours nearby, the ones with lots of recent, positive reviews show up first and win the customer. Reviews are one of the highest-impact, lowest-cost ways to grow. The best moment to ask is right after a happy customer has been served, while the good experience is fresh.

One important rule: Google and Yelp do NOT allow you to offer any reward — a discount, a freebie, anything — in exchange for a review. This is called an "incentivized review," and it can get all of your reviews removed and your listing penalized. So the safe, effective approach is simply to ASK — "we'd love your feedback" — and share your review link. Add your links below and your assistant will have them ready to share when a customer wants to leave feedback.

Grow through referrals (coming soon): Word-of-mouth is how most small businesses grow, and referrals make it repeatable. Here's the safe way to do it — and this matters legally. There's a U.S. law (the TCPA) that makes it illegal to text people who never personally gave you permission, with penalties up to $1,500 per text. So your business must never text a stranger's number, even if a customer offers it. The safe design we're building for you works differently: after a customer pays, your assistant gives THEM a personal referral link. THEY share it with their own friends. When enough friends click or book, that customer earns a discount — and your business never texts anyone who didn't reach out first. This is a feature we're building carefully (with legal review before launch). For now, filling in your links above gets you ready for it.

⚡ Enable AI Auto-Reply Mode?

When enabled, Modern Management's AI will respond to tenant messages on your behalf without your review.

What this means:

  • The AI may occasionally make mistakes
  • Messages go out under your name and authority
  • You can review all sent messages in your inbox
  • For safety, emergency-keyword messages (fire, gas leak, threats, injuries, etc.) will always require your manual review and you'll be notified by SMS

By enabling auto-reply, you accept responsibility for messages sent on your behalf.
